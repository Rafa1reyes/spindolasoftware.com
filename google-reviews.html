// STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Does my customer need to download an app?

No. iPhones from the XR and XS (2018) onward read the tag with the screen on — nothing to install, nothing to open. Android phones read it with NFC switched on, which it is by default on most phones. Nothing to sign into either way.

What if someone has an older phone?

A small number of older phones won't tap, and they'll just carry on with their day — the sign costs you nothing on the times it isn't used. Everyone else taps. I'd rather tell you that up front than have you find out on the counter.

Do you need my Google password?

Never. I don't want it and I won't ask for it. You copy the link out of your own Business Profile and send it to me — it's the same link you'd text a customer, and it's the only thing I need. I never sign in to anything of yours, at any point.

Where do I find the "Ask for reviews" link?

Search your own business name on Google while you're signed in as the owner. Your Business Profile controls appear, with a button to ask for reviews — that gives you a short link to copy. Paste it into the order and you're done. If you can't find it, just send me your business name and I'll help you get it.

What if I don't have a Google Business Profile yet?

Say so when you book and I'll walk you through starting one — it's free, and it's the single most valuable thing a local business can do online. Google verifies new profiles on their own schedule, so we may install the placard first and point it at your profile the day it goes live.

Does it need power, batteries or Wi-Fi?

None of the three. There is nothing to charge and nothing to connect. The chip is powered by the phone that taps it, which is why it will still work in ten years.

Can I move it after it's installed?

Yes — pick it up and put it wherever you like. It's a free-standing placard, not a fixture, and the chip doesn't care where it sits.

What if it stops working?

Call me and I'll re-program or replace it free. These chips have no moving parts and no battery, so it's rare — but "rare" isn't "never," and it's my name on it.

What if my review link changes?

The chip can be re-written as many times as you like — it isn't printed on, it's stored on it. If you change your business name, move to a new address, or want the sign to open something different, bring it to me or I'll swing by and re-write it. Free, forever, for anyone who has bought from me once.

Where do you install, and how soon?

I cover Sacramento and the Bay Area, in person. Send me your address when you order and I'll confirm the visit — usually within a few days. If you're further out than I can reasonably drive, I'll refund you in full the same day rather than leave you waiting on me.

Is buying reviews part of this?

No, and I wouldn't take that job. This makes it effortless for your real customers to leave their honest opinion. Google is good at spotting fake reviews and the penalty lands on your profile, not on the person who sold them.
