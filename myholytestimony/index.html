MY HOLYTESTIMONY
HOW CHRIST CHANGED MY STORY

Tell how Christ changed your life — out loud or typed — and add your photos. My Holy Testimony turns it into one master prompt you paste into an AI movie maker to build a film of your testimony, plus your testimony in plain words to share.

How it works

1

Answer questions about your walk with Christ. How the seed was planted, the years before, the moment it changed, the miracles you have witnessed, and what He has done since. Tap 🎤 and just talk — it keeps listening while you pause to think. Skip anything you want.

2

Add photos to any answer. My Holy Testimony reads each photo’s date to work out how old you were and matches the look of that era.

3

Name the people in it. Your parents, a godparent, your pastor, your spouse, your child — describe them and add their photos so they look right on screen.

4

Get your film prompt. One tap copies a complete film brief — plus a kit with your photos and your written testimony — ready for an AI movie app, your church or your family.

🔒 Private by design

Your story and photos stay on this device. There’s no account, and nothing is uploaded unless you copy or share it yourself. Use Back up my story in Settings to keep a copy safe.
