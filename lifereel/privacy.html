LIFE REEL

Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026 · Life Reel is published by Spindola Software.

The short version: your story and your photos stay on your device. Life Reel has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never receive, see, sell or share what you write or the photos you add.

What Life Reel stores, and where

Everything you put into Life Reel — your answers, your big moments, the people in your cast, your photos and the details you add to them, and your settings — is saved only on the device you are using, inside the app’s own storage (browser storage and IndexedDB). It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos you add are resized and read on your device: Life Reel looks at the date the camera stored in the file and at the colors of the picture (for example, black-and-white or faded) so your movie prompt can match the era. That processing happens entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Spindola Software does not collect personal information through Life Reel. There is no sign-up, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Like any website, the app’s files are downloaded from our web host (GitHub Pages, at spindolasoftware.com) when you first open Life Reel or when it updates. The host may record standard technical information such as your IP address when it delivers those files, under GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not use those records.

Voice typing

When you tap the microphone, Life Reel uses the speech-recognition service built into your device or browser (on Android and Chrome this is provided by Google) to turn your speech into text. Your audio is handled by that service under its own privacy policy. Life Reel only receives the resulting text, which it saves on your device like anything you type. You never have to use the microphone — typing works the same.

When you copy, share or save

Life Reel creates your movie prompt and Movie Kit on your device. They leave your device only when you copy, share or save them — for example when you paste the prompt into an AI video app or share your photos to another app. Whatever you send is then governed by that app’s own terms and privacy policy. Your prompt contains personal details about you and the people in your life, so think about who you share it with.

Backups

“Back up my story” saves a file containing your answers and photos to your device’s Downloads. Where you keep that file is up to you. Restoring a backup reads it back into Life Reel on your device.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Start over → Erase everything to delete every answer and photo from Life Reel on that device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data also deletes it. Because we never hold a copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side — and nothing we could restore if you erase it without a backup.

Children

Life Reel is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If Life Reel ever starts collecting any data, this page will say exactly what and why before that happens.
